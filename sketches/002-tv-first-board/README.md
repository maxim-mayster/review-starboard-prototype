## Variant: TV-first board

### Design stance
A large-format leaderboard meant to stay on a shop TV and keep the competition visible all month.

### Key choices
- Layout: oversized hero title, top-3 podium, full ranking on the side
- Typography: extra-large so it reads from across a room
- Color: dark high-contrast display with gold star emphasis
- Interaction: store toggle plus demo star increment for quick visual testing

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: motivation, visibility, team excitement
- Weak at: no admin logging tools on the same screen

### Best for
- A break room, dispatch area, or office TV that continuously shows monthly standings
